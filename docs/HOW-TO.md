# Dentora — how to do everything

The same guide is inside the app under **Practice → Help**, with a support form at the bottom.
Support: **070 657 7733** (Mon–Fri 08:30–18:00) · hello@dentora.ie

## Getting started
1. Settings → Practice details: name, address, phone (used on invoices, letters and texts).
2. Settings → Team: add everyone who needs a login. Clinicians get a diary column automatically.
3. Settings → Surgeries and Rota: name rooms, set who works where on which days.
4. Settings → Fee schedule: pre-filled Irish private schedule; click a price to change it.
5. Patients → Import CSV, or + New patient. Name and phone is enough.

Tip: add a visiting hygienist or locum without a login from Settings → Clinicians → Add (no login).

## Diary
- Click an empty slot to book; click an appointment to change status (Booked → Confirmed → Arrived → Completed, or Cancelled / FTA).
- Overlapping times for the same clinician are refused by the system.
- "absence" in a column header cancels that clinician's day and texts every patient a rebooking link.
- [Online] / [Video] prefixes mark bookings patients made themselves.

## Patients and records
- Search by name, phone or email; filter by scheme.
- Set each patient's recall cycle (3/6/12 months or off) on their record.
- Archive rather than delete. Medical alerts are only changed by a clinician; patient-submitted histories wait in an amber panel for review.
- Bulk SMS texts a filtered list.

## Dental chart, BPE and perio
- Click a tooth → condition, surfaces (M O D B L, R = root), status → Add.
- Entries are never edited or deleted. **Retire** with a reason; the "As of" dropdown shows the chart as it stood on any date.
- BPE: 0–4 / X per sextant, star for furcation. A 4, or a 3 in two sextants, flags a comprehensive perio exam.
- Perio chart: pocket depth, recession, plaque per site; attachment loss is calculated; past exams kept.

## Clinical notes
- Pick author, insert a template (Settings → Note templates), type or Dictate (Chrome/Edge).
- Saved notes cannot be changed — add a new note to correct.

## Treatment plans
- New plan → items from the fee schedule, adjust price/tooth → Proposed → Accepted → tick items as done.

## Billing, payments and debt
- New invoice → items → raise. Take payment by card/cash/transfer/insurance; status updates itself.
- Debt manager: balances by age, reminders sent, Send reminder (Account template), Write off (recorded per invoice, excluded from revenue).

## Recalls and reminders
- Completed visits schedule the next recall from the patient's cycle. Texts go one week and one day before; appointment reminders the day before.
- Settings → Message templates: {name} {clinic} {link} {phone} {type} {time} {amount}.
- Owners/admins can Run automation now; it also runs daily at 08:00.
- Until a Twilio number is connected, messages are logged as [demo] in the patient's Comms tab.

## Lab work
- New case: patient, lab, item, shade, sent, due back. Mark Received, then Fitted. Overdue cases are flagged.

## Referrals
- New referral: patient, specialist, reason, urgency → letter generated with history and scheme. Add your own specialists to the register.

## Tablet check-in
1. Once: open the kiosk link (shown on the Check-in page) on the waiting-room tablet. The tablet is never signed in.
2. Per patient: Check-in → click the patient → a 6-digit code appears → hand over the tablet.
3. The patient enters the code, answers, signs. The code works once and expires in 30 minutes.
4. Answers appear on the record as "awaiting review"; a clinician updates the alerts or marks reviewed.

## Handover sheet
- Nurse: New sheet chairside (visit type, findings, treatment, products, next visit). Front desk processes at checkout: next booking, invoice, mark processed.

## Patient portal and video
- Patients choose "I'm a patient", register with an email code, pick practice and clinician, see live slots (rota, 09:00–17:00, half-hourly).
- Video bookings get a private room; open the appointment → video button. Rooms open 15 minutes before the start.

## Orthodontics add-on (+€129/mo)
- Orthodontics → **+ Add patient** (top right, and beside the search box) → pick or create the patient → appliance, status, dates, IOTN, overjet/overbite → fee, deposit, instalment × count (schedule generated).
- Log visit: archwires / elastics / aligner tray, weeks to next visit. Record instalments from the Instalments due list.
- Side lists: Adjustments due (within 7 days or never seen) and Instalments due (next 30 days, overdue flagged).

## Endodontics add-on (+€89/mo)
- Endodontics → **+ Add patient** → pick or create → tooth (FDI) pre-fills canals → diagnosis, tests, per-canal WL / reference / MAF / obturation, medicament, sealer, dam, complications.
- Start / Complete / Sign off from the table. Set a review date → Reviews due. Enter the referring dentist → Report letter when complete.

## Team, roles and settings
- Owner/Admin: practice settings, fees, rooms, rota, templates, add-ons, team. Dentist/Staff: everything clinical and front desk.
- Only an owner can change another owner. Add-ons switch on/off immediately from Settings.
- Imaging: choose Romexis or CS Imaging; install the bridge (bridge/ folder) on each Windows PC.

## Reports
- Revenue collected (cash received), written off (separate), appointment outcomes and FTA rate, top billed treatments.
