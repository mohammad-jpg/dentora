## Everything Dentora does (short)

Front desk: rota-driven diary with rooms and leave, statuses and drag-reschedule, absence rebooking, day-before and recall texts (D-7, D-1), recall cycles, tablet check-in, handover sheet, tasks.
Clinical: FDI odontogram with history, BPE + perio chart, notes with templates and dictation, treatment plans, imaging refs and uploads with Romexis/CS Imaging hand-off, referral letters, lab tracking.
Billing: invoices from fee schedule, payments (card/cash/transfer/insurance/write-off), debt manager with ageing and templated reminders, PRSI/medical-card flags, reports.
Practice: multi-clinic with roles, self-serve signup with email codes, patient portal (booking, video, medical history), bulk SMS and templates, CSV import, mobile-friendly.
Orthodontics add-on: fixed/aligner/functional/retention cases, IOTN/overjet/overbite, archwire and tray visit log, auto instalment schedules with overdue tracking, work queues.
Endodontics add-on: per-tooth diagnosis and tests, per-canal WL/MAF/obturation, medicament/sealer/dam/complications, review scheduling, one-click referrer report.
